The Ecological Emergency

Bengaluru's Broken Water Governance Loop

01

Unabated Effluent & Foam

Over 40% of Bengaluru's wastewater enters lakes untreated. Iconic lakes like Bellandur & Varthur regularly erupt in toxic chemical foam and hypoxic fish kills.

02

Fragmented Citizen Grievance

Citizens report issues across Twitter, WhatsApp, and fragmented portals with zero GPS verification, leading to complaints being dismissed as unverified noise.

03

Zero Statutory Accountability

Ward authorities auto-close tickets without verifiable remediation proof. No formal linkage exists to Section 24 of the Water Act or Central CPGRAMS oversight.

Signature Innovation

Dynamic Algorithmic Lake Health Score

Score = round(100 - min(100, 8 × ∑(severity_i × e^(-ageDays_i / 14)))) - Modifiers
  • ✓ 90-Day Exponential Decay: Recent severe pollution heavily penalizes health; remediated lakes recover over time.
  • ✓ Chemical Modifiers: Abnormal pH (<6 or >9) subtracts 5; Low Secchi transparency (<30cm) subtracts 5.
  • ✓ Community Corroboration: ≥2 citizens within 250m and 48h increases effective triage priority.

Technical Stack & Resiliency
Verification Pipeline

Anti-Spam & Multimodal AI Engine

dHash

Perceptual Duplicate Detection

Sharp resizes evidence to 9x8 grayscale, generating a 64-bit matrix. If an image has a Hamming distance ≤ 8 to any photo taken within 500m in the last 7 days, it is instantly marked REJECTED to prevent double counting.

GPS EXIF

Tamper-Proof Coordinate Guard

Coordinates are extracted directly from photo camera sensors with exifr. Coordinates can never be user-typed. Missing EXIF or capture skew > 15 min automatically tags reports as UNVERIFIED.

Weather

Stormwater Runoff Correlation

Open-Meteo 72h precipitation analysis flags whether rainfall exceeded 20mm prior to the report, automatically differentiating illegal dry-weather sewage dumping from wet-weather municipal stormwater overflow.

Governance Engine

Statutory State Machine & SLA Escalation

SUBMITTED → AI_VALIDATED → COMPLAINT_SENT → ACKNOWLEDGED → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED_CLAIMED → CITIZEN_VERIFIED → CLOSED
Level 1 Escalation (> 7 Days)

Triggered if complaint remains unacknowledged by Ward Executive Engineer for over 7 business days.

Level 2 Escalation (> 14 Days)

Triggered if remediation is not in progress within 14 days. Escalated to Zonal Joint Commissioner, BBMP.

Level 3 Escalation (> 21 Days)

Statutory breach escalated to Member Secretary, Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) for legal prosecution.

Walkthrough

5-Minute Judge Demo Script

Step 1 • Citizen

Report Incident

Open /report. Click "Toxic Foam (Bellandur)". Watch EXIF GPS lock coordinates onto mini-map. Record pH 9.0 and Secchi <30cm. Submit report.

Step 2 • AI Engine

Validate & Notice

AI assigns Severity 4/5. dHash flags image uniqueness. Download official PDF complaint letter with Tracking ID (e.g. JD-BLR-202611-1007).

Step 3 • Official

Triage & Action

Switch to official@demo.in at /login. View SLA queue. Click "Acknowledge" → "In Progress" → "Mark Action Taken" with resolution notes.

Step 4 • Citizen

Verify & Close

Back to /my-reports as Citizen. Inspect audit timeline. Click "Verify Remediation" to permanently close the case with citizen on-ground validation!
