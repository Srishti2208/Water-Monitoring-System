The Problem Statement

Bengaluru's Broken Water Governance Loop

01

Chronic Effluents & Toxic Froth

Over 1,400 MLD of untreated municipal sewage discharges directly into lakes. Iconic waterbodies like Bellandur & Varthur routinely erupt in toxic chemical foam and hypoxic fish mortality events.

02

Unverified Complaints & Spam

Grievance portals accept unverified text with no camera GPS validation. Civic officers are flooded with fake reports or identical duplicate spam, creating fatigue and delaying genuine emergencies.

03

Ghost Closures Without Action

Municipal officials mark tickets "Resolved" on paper to meet administrative SLA quotas, without performing desiltation or weed cleanup. Citizens have zero mechanism to dispute fraudulent closures.

Problem Context: Urban Limnology & Municipal E-Governance Gaps
Project Mission

Core Objectives of JalDrishti

Objective 1

Tamper-Proof Citizen Science

Eliminate fake reports by extracting hardware EXIF GPS coordinates and timestamps directly from camera sensors on the client side, disallowing manual coordinate tampering.

Objective 2

Generative AI Multimodal Triaging

Use Multimodal Generative LLMs (Gemini 1.5) to visually classify pollution categories (foam, sewage, dead fish, algae) and generate structured, calibrated severity rankings (1–5) in real time.

Objective 3

Dynamic 0–100 Lake Health Index

Formulate a continuous environmental health algorithm incorporating exponential temporal decay ($\lambda = 14$ days), rainfall context, and physical readings (pH, turbidity, temp).

Objective 4

Statutory Enforcement & Citizen Sign-off

Draft legal notices under Section 24 of the Water Act, 1974, enforce automated 15-minute SLA cron escalations, and require citizen on-ground verification before cases can be closed.

Verification Pipeline

Anti-Spam & Hardware Verification

dHash

64-Bit Perceptual Duplicate Hash

Sharp resizes evidence to 9x8 greyscale, computing horizontal brightness gradients. If Hamming distance $D_H \le 8$ vs. any report within 500m in the last 7 days, it is flagged as a duplicate to prevent artificial metric skewing.

Hamming Distance: D_H ≤ 8 (64-bit gradient)
EXIF GPS

Tamper-Proof Coordinate Guard

Coordinates are extracted directly from hardware camera EXIF headers using exifr. Manual coordinate typing is strictly disallowed. Missing GPS or capture-to-upload skew >15 min automatically tags the report UNVERIFIED.

Hardware Sensor Metadata • Zero Spoofing
Open-Meteo

Precipitation Weather Context

Fetches 72-hour precipitation data at report coordinates. If recent rainfall $\ge 20\text{mm}$, the system tags RAIN_CONTEXT to ensure natural stormwater runoff isn't falsely penalized as an industrial breach.

72h Archival Precipitation Telemetry

Mathematical Model

Dynamic Lake Health Score (0–100)

Algorithmic Formulation
$$\text{Score} = \text{round}\left(100 - \min\left(100, 8 \sum_{i=1}^{n} S_i \cdot e^{-\frac{\Delta t_i}{14}}\right)\right) - \Delta_{\text{mod}}$$
  • ✓ Exponential Decay ($\lambda = 14$ days): Recent severe violations heavily depress score; remediated waterbodies naturally recover over time.
  • ✓ Sensory Modifiers ($\Delta_{\text{mod}}$): Abnormal pH (<6 or >9) subtracts 5 points; Secchi transparency <30cm subtracts 5 points.
  • ✓ Community Corroboration: $\ge 2$ distinct citizens reporting within 250m & 48h bumps triage severity and triggers community validation badging.

Administrative Governance

Statutory State Machine & SLA Escalation

SUBMITTED → AI_VALIDATED → COMPLAINT_SENT → ACKNOWLEDGED → IN_PROGRESS → RESOLVED_CLAIMED → CITIZEN_VERIFIED → CLOSED
Level 1 Escalation (> 7 Days)

Triggered automatically by the background cron if a complaint remains unacknowledged by the Ward Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE) for 7 days.

Level 2 Escalation (> 14 Days)

Escalates directly to the Zonal Joint Commissioner if remediation is not placed "In Progress" within 14 days, bumping priority to URGENT.

Level 3 Statutory Breach (> 21 Days)

Notice forwarded directly to the Member Secretary of the Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) for judicial action under the Water Act.

Accountability Gate

The Citizen-Verified Closure Protocol

Eliminating "Ghost Closures"

In typical government portals, civic officers mark complaints "Resolved" without doing physical work. JalDrishti fundamentally alters this dynamic by withholding case finality until the reporting citizen conducts an on-ground audit.

👷
Step 1: Official Claims Action Taken

Ward officer enters resolution notes. Ticket state moves to RESOLVED_CLAIMED.

📸
Step 2: Citizen On-Ground Sign-Off

Original reporter inspects site. Clicking Verify Remediation closes the case permanently.

⚠️
Alternative: Dispute & Reopen

If cleanup is fraudulent, citizen clicks Dispute. Ticket transitions to REOPENED with an immediate Level 2 escalation!

100%

Citizen Authority

Ward officers cannot unilaterally mark a case permanently closed. Public ward rankings on /accountability track the percentage of complaints citizen-verified vs. contested.
